## Grassmannian
Definition of Grassmannian:Fix $N=n+k$,the Grassmannian $Gr_n(\mathbb{R}^N)$
$$
\{X\subset\mathbb{R}^N|X\}
$$
is a dim $n$ linear subspace of dimension $n$.The Stiefel space of $n$ frames
$$
V_n(\mathbb{R}^N)=\text{n independent $\mathbb{R}^N$ vectors}
$$
We have a natural projection map $\pi:V_n(\mathbb{R}^N)\to Gr_n(\mathbb{R}^N)$.
We can also define the Orthonomal frames $V_n^o(\mathbb{R}^N)$.The quotient topology induced by $V_n(\mathbb{R}^N)$ and $V_n^o(\mathbb{R}^N)$.

Lemma:The space $Gr_n(\mathbb{R}^{n+k})$ is a compact topological manifold and the orthonormal complement defines a homeo.
